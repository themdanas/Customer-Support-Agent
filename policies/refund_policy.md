# Refund Policy

## Standard Refund Window

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery for any item
returned in its original, unused condition with packaging intact. Refund
requests made after 30 days from the delivery date are not eligible under
standard policy and must be escalated to a human agent for case-by-case
review.

## Refund Amount Limits

Refund requests of $75 or less may be automatically approved and processed
without human review, provided the order is within the 30-day window and the
refund amount does not exceed 80% of the order's total value. Any refund
request exceeding $75, or exceeding 80% of the order total, must be routed
to a human agent for approval before processing.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If an item arrives damaged or defective, the customer is entitled to a full
refund or replacement regardless of the standard 30-day window, provided the
issue is reported within 14 days of delivery. Photographic evidence should be
requested from the customer before approving a damaged-item refund.

## Wrong Item Received

If a customer received an item different from what they ordered, a full
refund or a correct replacement shipment should be offered at no additional
cost to the customer. This case does not count against the standard refund
window.

## Non-Refundable Items

Gift cards, digital downloads, and clearance items marked "final sale" are
not eligible for refunds under any circumstances. If a customer requests a
refund for one of these categories, explain the exclusion and do not
process a refund, but do offer store credit as a goodwill alternative if the
item is defective.

## Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method within
5-7 business days of approval. Store credit, when issued, is applied
immediately.
