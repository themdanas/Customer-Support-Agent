# Shipping Policy

## Standard Shipping Times

Standard shipping typically takes 3-7 business days from the order date.
Expedited shipping, when selected at checkout, takes 1-2 business days.
Delivery estimates are provided at checkout and reflected in the order's
expected delivery date.

## Delayed Shipments

If an order has not arrived within 10 business days of the order date and
tracking shows no movement for more than 3 consecutive days, treat this as
a delayed shipment. Offer the customer a status update using the latest
tracking information, and if the delay exceeds 14 business days with no
resolution, offer a full refund or reshipment at no cost.

## Address Changes After Order Placed

A shipping address can be changed only if the order status is still
"pending" and has not yet moved to "shipped." Once an order has shipped, the
address cannot be changed, and the customer should be advised to contact the
carrier directly or to refuse delivery so the package is returned to sender.

## Lost Packages

A package is considered lost if tracking has shown no update for more than
10 business days and the estimated delivery date has passed by more than 5
business days. Lost package claims should be escalated to a human agent
rather than automatically refunded, since carrier investigation may be
required before the claim is confirmed.

## International Shipping

International orders may be subject to customs delays outside of the
company's control. Customs delays are not eligible for delay-related refunds
under this policy, but the customer should still be kept informed of
tracking status.
